Delete your Plotika account

Plotika · live.plotly.app · Last updated 23 September 2026

You can permanently delete your Plotika account and the data associated with it. There are two ways to do this — both result in the same permanent deletion.

Option 1 — Delete directly in the app (fastest)

Open Plotika and go to Profile → Settings → Delete account. Confirm when prompted. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out on every device.

Option 2 — Request deletion from this page

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot sign in, submit the form below. We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days.

This cannot be undone. Deleting your account permanently removes your learning history, saved stories and notes. It does not automatically cancel a paid subscription — cancel that separately in the App Store or Google Play.

Use the address you signed in with. If you used Sign in with Apple and hid your email, enter the @privaterelay.appleid.com address Apple gave you.

You will receive a confirmation email once your request is received.
